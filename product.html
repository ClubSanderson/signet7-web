One platform. One managed trust framework.

Signet7 combines cryptographic evidence for protected email with the Verifiable Sender Network's managed enrollment, discovery, status, lifecycle, scalable verification, and enterprise administration.

Core email capability

Declare coverage. Verify the evidence.

The intended product flow begins with an important email—not an agent action.

01

Prepare

Normalize a versioned set of message components and declare exactly what the signature covers.

02

Sign

Bind the covered representation to an authorized key and relevant sender or organization context.

03

Verify

Resolve key/status trust, detect changes to covered components, and produce an explicit result.

04

Preserve

Create tamper-evident evidence with signer, coverage, result, chain, and custody metadata.

05

Export

Allow later validation with separately controlled signer, tenant, and expected-head inputs.

06

Integrate

Connect providers, gateways, recipient systems, archives, evidence tools, AI-agent policy gates, workflow automation, compliance pipelines, ERP/CRM systems, and enterprise records consumers.

Verifiable Sender Network

The managed trust infrastructure.

VSN establishes and maintains the organizational relationships, key status, lifecycle governance, scalable verification, and administration that make Signet7's cryptographic evidence model usable at enterprise scale.

EnrollmentOrganization, sender, administrator, and key relationships.
DiscoveryManaged resolution of key and trust status.
LifecycleRotation, revocation, recovery, expiry, and audit history.
EnterprisePolicy, administration, scale, monitoring, support, and abuse controls.
Status: these are approved product-direction capabilities, not a claim that the complete managed service is operating today.
Platform model

Cryptographic evidence and managed trust, operating together.

Protected evidence

Defined message coverage, cryptographic signing, explicit verification results, and portable records.

Complete MIME and attachment coverage remains email-MVP work.

Managed VSN trust

Organizational enrollment, discovery, status, rotation, revocation, lifecycle governance, and scalable verification.

The complete managed service remains planned.

Enterprise operations

Administration, integrations, evidence operations, support, audit, and organization-specific controls.

No price, SLA, or final service commitment is approved.

Platform principle: verification remains portable and independently checkable, while VSN supplies the managed organizational trust needed at scale. Technical interoperability is not positioned as a complete substitute for VSN.

Coverage boundary

No protection claim without an exact coverage declaration.

Current exact input

  • Normalized sender.
  • Message identifier.
  • Message body string.
  • Optional exact action in the extension profile.

Not yet complete

  • Full MIME header/body canonicalization.
  • Comprehensive attachments and nested MIME parts.
  • Provider-specific transformations and rendering.
  • Recipient inbox indicator and explanation UX.
