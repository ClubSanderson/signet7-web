Signet7 is building a secure email trust platform for consequential communications. It seals declared protected message components with cryptographic evidence, helps recipients evaluate sender/key provenance and integrity, and preserves portable records for future verification. The Verifiable Sender Network is the planned managed trust infrastructure that brings the model to enterprise scale.
