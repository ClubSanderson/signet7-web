Covered verification

What the verifier checks.

01

Canonical record bytes

Recompute the covered record representation rather than trusting presentation order.

02

Hash-chain relationship

Validate sequence, genesis requirements and previous-hash linkage across covered entries.

03

Signature + signer trust

Verify signatures and separately evaluate signer identity against supplied trusted IDs.

04

Expected tenant + head

Compare tenant and covered head with independently controlled expected values when supplied.

It cannot establish alone

Completeness is not self-proving.

  • No later or unrecorded event exists
  • The caller enforced the verdict
  • The underlying input was truthful
  • The signer had legal authority
  • Every administrator/provider is non-malicious
  • A law, control or evidentiary rule is satisfied

Language boundary

Supported wording

  • Signed and hash-chained

Not established

  • Not tamper-proof or universally immutable
  • Not secure, vulnerability-free or zero-risk
  • Not independently audited or penetration-tested
  • Not certified or compliant
  • Not live, production-ready or customer-proven
  • Not court-admissible or regulator approved
