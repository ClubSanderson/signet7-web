Make consequential email self-proving.

Email is where the world's most consequential decisions happen, yet it still cannot be trusted on its own. Signet7 fixes that. The product center

Trust that travels with the evidence.

Signet7 is intended to complement provider, transport, gateway, reputation, and user controls with explicit sender/key trust and portable integrity evidence.

01

Establish provenance

Connect the covered email to a signing key and a separately governed sender or organization trust relationship.

02

Detect covered changes

Verify the message components the coverage profile explicitly protects—without pretending unspecified content is included.

03

Preserve evidence

Export signed, tamper-evident records for later validation with independently controlled trust and completeness inputs.

Why now

The regulatory need is already taking shape.

SEC recordkeeping rules require certain regulated firms to preserve and produce electronic records with integrity controls. SOX-related audit rules require covered audit and review records to be retained for seven years. HIPAA requires specified documentation to be retained for six years, while the EU AI Act introduces logging and retention obligations for covered high-risk AI systems as its requirements take effect.

The boundary: these regimes do not mandate Signet7 or make every email a regulated record. They expose a recurring business requirement: organizations must preserve important records, demonstrate their integrity, and produce credible evidence when regulators, auditors, courts, insurers, or counterparties demand it.
The name

The name defines the mission.

A signet is a seal of authenticity and proof. The 7 represents the seven-year audit-retention benchmark that shaped Signet7's long-term evidence standard.

Built for consequential records

Wire approvals, contracts, account changes, tax records, compliance notices, and executive directives can be examined or challenged years after they were sent.

Verifiable Sender Network

Managed trust is where the network earns its value.

VSN is the planned managed trust infrastructure within Signet7. It is intended to establish and maintain verified relationships among organizations, senders, and cryptographic keys while providing lifecycle and administration at scale.

Current candidate

Source-built capability. Live gates still absent.

Implemented foundation

  • s7-email-1 signing and verification for declared headers, MIME structure, plain/HTML bodies, and attachments.
  • Tenant-bound or optional DNS key trust, component results, replay/time checks, and recipient verification UI/API.
  • A signed, hash-chained evidence ledger with export, detached verification, and recovery tooling.
  • CLI email tooling, Gmail pilot tooling, SES send enforcement, packaging, and manual release/deployment workflow source.

Not established yet

  • A deployed inbound mailbox gateway, agent inbox service, or universal receive pipeline.
  • No live Gmail/SES qualification, public SDK/API/connector program, or deployed downstream integration.
  • A complete operated VSN, live GitHub controls, or approved production AWS deployment.
  • A release, deployed service, production qualification, SLA, customer pilot, certification, universal legal identity, or universal retention.
Activation boundary: authenticated physical epochs are source-built, disabled by default, and not activated. “Tamper-evident” means covered changes can be detected; it does not mean every copy is impossible to delete, every history is complete, or every real-world fact is proven.
Supporting extension

Verified email can inform automated action.

AI agents, enterprise systems, workflow automation, ERP/CRM, and records systems are intended consumers of Signet7 evidence—not live integrations. The current action-bound profile and MCP tooling support evaluation, but the integrating caller remains responsible for enforcement.

Email first

The trust result begins with the email. Automation is one possible consumer—not the definition of the platform.
