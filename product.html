Coverage boundary

No protection claim without an exact coverage declaration.

Current source-built coverage

  • Protected sender/routing headers including From, Message-ID, Date, Subject, and present recipient fields.
  • Declared MIME tree, leaf-part order, media type, disposition, and component metadata.
  • Plain-text/HTML content and attachment bytes/filenames under s7-email-1.
  • Component-level results, resource limits, time/replay signals, and optional exact action in the separate extension profile.

Not established by source alone

  • Universal support for every email structure, transformation, client, gateway, or provider.
  • No live Gmail/SES transformation and interoperability qualification.
  • A deployed receive pipeline, mailbox indicator, or recipient-side service.
  • Independent security acceptance, production readiness, certification, or legal conclusion.
